## Why

Day 1 patrols could only hurt a player by touching them. Strafing, choosing cover and firing a rocket beside a group should change the outcome of an encounter.

## What Changes

Increase mission movement speed. Give armed patrols readable shots that lock aim during windup and can be dodged. Add solid freight crates, a quick kick, splash on floor impacts, personal health and armor, and two shared access cards that bypass reinforcements.
