# Let the visitors show what they are doing

## 1. A footprint at wake-up (VIS-01)

- [ ] 1.1 Mark each visitor’s first appearance on its actual wake beat using the defaults in the matching requirement. Watch the five staggered wake times and join while three visitors are already awake.

## 2. Snow on your boots (VIS-02)

- [ ] 2.1 Keep the cause of slowed movement readable beside another message using the defaults in the matching requirement. Brush a visitor at a numbered door and retrigger contact. Keep both messages legible in grayscale.

## 3. A clear end to the slowdown (VIS-03)

- [ ] 3.1 Signal the instant a player regains normal walking speed using the defaults in the matching requirement. Hold contact for three refreshes, step away for 1.3 s and count one recovery.

## 4. A visitor approaching from behind (VIS-04)

- [ ] 4.1 Give an approaching visitor a directional cue using the defaults in the matching requirement. Approach with two visitors from behind, then turn to face them and test the rearm distance.

## 5. Snowman scarf follow-through (VIS-05)

- [ ] 5.1 Let the snowman’s scarf follow its turn using the defaults in the matching requirement. Check a quarter turn from each gallery and inspect the scarf in grayscale.

## 6. Gingerbread crumbs on a skip (VIS-06)

- [ ] 6.1 Match gingerbread crumbs to the skipper’s gait using the defaults in the matching requirement. Capture three gait cycles near the carpet and on stone and align the crumbs to the actual beats.

## 7. A penguin skate scratch (VIS-07)

- [ ] 7.1 Give the skater a trace at changes of direction using the defaults in the matching requirement. Record two weave reversals and a stopped skater, then check scratch direction and count.

## 8. Clockwork quarter beats (VIS-08)

- [ ] 8.1 Let the clockwork guard’s actual turns make a sound using the defaults in the matching requirement. Turn the guard across the 0° boundary and move past the audible limit.

## 9. Frost Wisp drift (VIS-09)

- [ ] 9.1 Make the wisp’s floating motion readable against the hall using the defaults in the matching requirement. Check the rising and falling phases against the tree and against a dark gallery.

## 10. A visitor bows out (VIS-10)

- [ ] 10.1 Give cleared visitors a brief material-specific farewell using the defaults in the matching requirement. Clear each species and confirm that late join, a duplicate hit and a later wave cannot replay the earlier exit.

## 11. Tune in play and verify controls

- [ ] 11.1 Watch a single arrival wave from the centre aisle, cross each visitor’s path and let the last one leave. Repeat beside a locked door and compare contact/recovery timings with presentation disabled.
- [ ] 11.2 Mute audio, reduce Detail, set Motion to zero and disable captions in turn. Keep a persistent legible slow-state indicator when movement is affected, on both flat screens and in stereo.
- [ ] 11.3 Run the affected C and Lua unit suites with their separate 95% gates and the relevant automated playthroughs. Record measured cues, negative cases and platform or device gaps with the change.
