## Why

The overhead directional light gives opposite sides of the hall the same daylight. The west fireplace reads as a slab, and the tree's square tiers hide its decorations from the entry aisle.

## What Changes

- Light the galleries with visible candles, strings of bulbs, the decorated tree and the hearth.
- Let the hearth and entrance chandelier cast shadows. Keep the passages between fixtures dark.
- Open the upper and low corner windows onto a fixed star field, snowy ridges and a closer tree line.
- Build a Christmas mantel and give its log fire a changing silhouette and glowing coal bed.
- Replace the square tree boughs with layered spruce branches and colored glass lights.
- Keep the loose asset pipeline, reduced-motion behavior and multiplayer routes.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `hall-lighting` covers practical lighting, selective shadows, the winter windows, the Christmas hearth and the decorated spruce.

## Impact

Updates the game art generator, external assets, hall renderer setup, presentation tests and release captures.
