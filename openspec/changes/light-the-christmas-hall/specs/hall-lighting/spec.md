## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Practical hall lighting

The hall SHALL be lit by the fireplace, candles and visible electric strings, with local falloff and distinct warm and colored pools.

#### Scenario: Explore at night

- **WHEN** a player walks from the arrival aisle to the galleries
- **THEN** the hall uses point lights tied to visible fixtures, the door numbers remain legible and each group of bulbs retains its color.

#### Scenario: Walk through the entrance candlelight

- **WHEN** a visitor crosses the aisle or passes the hearth
- **THEN** its moving silhouette falls away from a casting fixture, and the other lights continue illuminating the floor underneath.
- **AND** static furnishings cast shadows from the log fire and entrance chandelier alone.

### Requirement: Winter windows

The game SHALL expose a starry winter landscape through the hall’s upper and eye-level windows.

#### Scenario: Look outside while moving sideways

- **WHEN** a player looks through a corner window and sidesteps
- **THEN** the rays to distant stars stay fixed as the window frame, fir trees and snow-capped ridges shift at different rates.

### Requirement: Christmas hearth and spruce

The hall SHALL contain a furnished mantel, an animated log fire and a layered evergreen with colored lights.

#### Scenario: Walk up to the west hearth

- **WHEN** the player approaches between the numbered doors
- **THEN** they see brick around a recessed log grate, mantel decorations, glowing embers and changing flame silhouettes.
- **AND** reduced motion provides a steady fire and illumination.

#### Scenario: Circle the Christmas tree

- **WHEN** the player views the tree from opposing aisles
- **THEN** branching foliage, hanging ornaments and colored light strings are visible from both sides.
