## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A readable main route through Tinseltown

The mission SHALL lead the group from the rooftop to Luna and the stadium with marked exits, short shared shootouts and single-action environmental puzzles.

#### Scenario: Clear a sector together

- **WHEN** the group enters the cinema, sorting dock, launch control or hatchery
- **THEN** the game identifies the current wave and surviving targets, and clearing the last wave enables the marked main-route control for everyone.

#### Scenario: Operate the diner and finish the stadium

- **WHEN** a player uses the frozen skater from either side
- **THEN** it slides into the till. At the stadium direct hits can stagger the Queen after the hatchlings are cleared and a shot at the cart finishes the mission.

### Requirement: Distinct mission weapons

The five mission weapons SHALL have original articulated models, distinct firing sounds and visible shot signatures, and retain the existing unlock order.

#### Scenario: Switch weapons in solo or a room

- **WHEN** a player selects an unlocked weapon
- **THEN** the first-person model and firing action identify the equipped tool, touch displays its silhouette, and reduced motion suppresses mechanical kick.
