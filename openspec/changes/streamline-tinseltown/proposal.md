## Why

Four long freight escorts interrupt the Earth-to-Moon chase. The first-person guns also share shapes with the hall snowcasters, making weapon switches hard to read.

## What Changes

Give each of the five tools original first-person art, a mechanical firing pose, a sound and a shot effect. Replace mandatory cargo routes with shootouts in four open-front sets on the main aisle. Keep the freeze, shrink and scenery puzzles, the flight, shared checkpoints and ornament. Change the room catalog revision so clients and hosts agree on the encounters.
