## Why

The opening weapon required two presses to deal damage. An usher could take a hit without breaking stride, and a defeated courier vanished immediately.

## What Changes

- Equip the Snow Pistol on entry and add a rooftop encounter. Make both stage rigs direct shooting targets.
- Animate hips, shoulders, skates and loader arms. Give the hatchlings a bounding sprite gait.
- Carry confirmed hits in room snapshots. Add a brief directional recoil, impact sounds, snow fragments and a distinct defeat pose.
- Use the existing motion, effects, sound, vibration, caption and hit-marker controls.

## Impact

Day 1 Lua, loose voxel assets, browser tool labels, mission tests and the room content revision change. Players retain their saved ornaments.
