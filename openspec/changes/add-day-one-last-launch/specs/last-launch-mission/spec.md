# Last Launch from Tinseltown

## Purpose

Give friends a single Day 1 chase through a Christmas cinema, a rocket flight and a lunar alien hatchery, with a shared payoff for recovering the stolen ornament.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Earth to Luna in one adventure

Door 1 SHALL take eligible players to the snowy Los Angeles cinema. The route SHALL connect the parcel depot, launch gantry, playable mail rocket, lunar surface and stadium hatchery in one mission. The crew SHALL control the launch and retain control of their view during flight.

#### Scenario: Ride the second rocket

- **WHEN** a player follows the stolen parcel to dispatch and clears the gantry
- **THEN** they see the parcel leave on the first rocket, board the second rocket, start the flight and walk out onto Luna in the same run.

#### Scenario: First visit

- **WHEN** a first-time player follows the marked route at ordinary difficulty
- **THEN** the pacing target from entering Door 1 to recovering the ornament is 25–35 minutes, with optional secrets off the required route.

### Requirement: Five distinct arcade weapons

Players SHALL have access along the route to a direct-fire Snow Pistol, a freezer, a single-shot Snow Rocket, a shrink ray and rapid twin launchers. Any tool needed to open the next passage SHALL be available again after a missed attempt.

#### Scenario: Fire on the first press

- **WHEN** a player aims the pistol at a vulnerable enemy and presses Fire
- **THEN** the room resolves one direct hit immediately. Holding Fire repeats at the pistol cadence. A single drag on the touch look area continues to aim.

#### Scenario: Change the outcome of a fight

- **WHEN** players try each tool on a suitable target
- **THEN** the freezer produces a pushable ice statue, the Snow Rocket bursts against a marked barrier, the shrink ray makes a large alien small enough to stomp, and the twin launchers sustain alternating small shots while Fire is held.

#### Scenario: Clear the diner before ringing the bell

- **WHEN** players have cleared the skaters or missed the bell with their last statue
- **THEN** they can freeze the reusable diner mascot, slide it into the bell and collect the dispatch ticket.

### Requirement: Shared checkpoints and solo access

One to eight players SHALL be able to finish every required objective. Unlocked doors, the rocket trip and the stadium fight SHALL advance once per active run. Later arrivals SHALL be able to join at its current checkpoint.

#### Scenario: Open both sides of the lunar sorter

- **WHEN** a solo player uses the parcel sizer, crosses the miniature sorting line and releases the freight door
- **THEN** they regain normal size and can continue. In co-op, the same action opens the full-size door for the rest of the crew.

#### Scenario: Catch up after launch

- **WHEN** a participant misses departure or a friend enters Door 1 after lunar landing
- **THEN** the participant can use the marked catch-up dispatch and the new arrival joins at the lunar checkpoint, with the same unlocked tools and objectives as the crew.

### Requirement: Recover the ornament

The lunar stadium SHALL contain one shared alien Queen encounter. Completing it SHALL recover the brass rocket ornament, award each present participant their Day 1 ornament once and open a route back to the hall.

#### Scenario: Bring down the scenery

- **WHEN** the crew exposes the lunar stage rig and shoots the striped target on the moving sideline cart
- **THEN** the Moon drops, the Queen is boxed for return, the crew recovers the ornament and the stadium tunnel opens to Door 1.

#### Scenario: Two players finish together

- **WHEN** two players trigger the finish during the same encounter or complete a later replay
- **THEN** each participating player has one Day 1 ornament and a friend who stayed in the hall keeps their existing collection.
