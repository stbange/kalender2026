# Day 1 · Last Launch from Tinseltown

## Why

The friends remember deciding exactly when to click Duke's pipe-bomb remote. Day 1 would give them that moment twice, first in a Los Angeles cinema and again in a football stadium on the Moon, with a stolen Christmas ornament to follow between them.

## What Changes

- Give Door 1 one continuous, roughly 30-minute adventure for one to eight players. Chase a parcel from a cinema through a diner and dispatch depot, ride a mail rocket to Luna, and recover the hall's brass rocket ornament from an alien hatchery.
- Make the single-shot Snow Rocket, remote snow crackers, freezer, shrink ray and twin launchers useful in different situations. Let ordinary objects such as a projector, parcel scale and scoreboard respond to the player.
- Bring the group back to the hall with the Day 1 ornament. The level design includes shared checkpoints, late entry, secrets and a first-run pacing target.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `last-launch-mission` covers the playable Earth-to-Luna route, its snow weapons and the shared lunar finale.

## Impact

The future pack would use the existing dated portal, room service, controls and ornament save. The current mission fixture supports a small shared objective. The proposed route needs movement bounds, encounters and checkpoint state across a much larger space. Original scenery, characters, sounds and script would ship as editable mission files. The [level design](design.md) gives the historical sources and a minute-by-minute route.
