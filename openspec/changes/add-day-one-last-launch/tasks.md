# Implementation tasks for Last Launch from Tinseltown

## 1. Make the route walkable

- [ ] 1.1 Stage a development copy of the Day 1 pack with simple geometry for all six stops. Walk from the cinema roof into the rocket cabin and out onto the lunar pitch in one run.
- [ ] 1.2 Give the pack movement bounds, firing and shared encounter updates through the existing room. Run a headless test that enters the mission, moves and fires in it, then returns and fires at a hall bell.
- [ ] 1.3 Synchronize the gantry, lunar dock and stadium checkpoints. Test two friends entering at different times, missing the flight, leaving independently and rejoining while the run is active.

## 2. Build the Earth chase and rocket

- [ ] 2.1 Build the projector reveal, prop cart and remote snow crackers. Unit-test remote ownership and distinct press/release actions. In the Web touch test, aim with one drag and time the pop with a fresh Fire press.
- [ ] 2.2 Build the diner, freezer, bell puzzle, reusable mascot and red dispatch ticket. Verify a first try and a retry after clearing all the skating boars both open the freight lift.
- [ ] 2.3 Stage the loader fight, first rocket's departure and crew-operated launch in the reserve rocket. Verify the heavy Snow Rocket breaks the loader's armor, a second launch request leaves one departure, and players keep control of their view during flight.

## 3. Build lunar customs and the Moon Bowl

- [ ] 3.1 Build the shrink ray, parcel sizer, miniature railway, regrowth and freight door. Test a solo crossing, a volunteer leaving mid-puzzle and a second player taking over at the sizer.
- [ ] 3.2 Build the incubator reveal, alternating twin launchers, Queen's charge and sideline cart. Exercise a missed remote and a successful finish. Confirm a simultaneous finish grants one ornament per participant, including on replay.
- [ ] 3.3 Author the original voxel scenery, enemy sprites, scoreboard, signs, music, cues and two optional interactions. Review the cinema and lunar reveals in-game, check captions and reduced motion, and credit each original asset under CC BY 4.0.

## 4. Playtime and release verification

- [ ] 4.1 Time first-time solo and co-op runs, including eight players. Record each arrival against the six time budgets and tune toward 25–35 minutes from Door 1 to the ornament.
- [ ] 4.2 Run the automated solo and networked game routes, the 95% first-party unit coverage checks and a packaged desktop, mobile Web and Quest 3 control pass. Verify the hall-only friend can keep playing throughout the mission.
- [ ] 4.3 For the playable release, register the Day 1 pack under one matching client and room revision, document it, and check December 1 entry, return and saved ornaments against the packaged build.
