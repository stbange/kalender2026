# Make each snowball impact legible

## 1. Personal hit mark (HIT-01)

- [ ] 1.1 Acknowledge a surviving visitor hit privately for its thrower using the defaults in the matching requirement. Compare A hitting a visitor with B throwing past it. Record a private mark only on A.

## 2. Visitor flinch (HIT-02)

- [ ] 2.1 Make a surviving hit visible on the visitor’s body using the defaults in the matching requirement. Test a three-HP snowman before and after a two-power pistol hit, including a second hit while the first pose settles.

## 3. Powder at the point of contact (HIT-03)

- [ ] 3.1 Locate a hit on the struck surface using the defaults in the matching requirement. Capture the contact on a moving visitor from two angles and inspect the capped burst.

## 4. Soft hit sound for the thrower (HIT-04)

- [ ] 4.1 Give a surviving hit its own short sound using the defaults in the matching requirement. Have one fan hit two visitors and verify one private thup on the throwing client.

## 5. A distinct finishing mark (HIT-05)

- [ ] 5.1 Distinguish a finishing shot from an ordinary hit by shape using the defaults in the matching requirement. Compare a finishing shot, simultaneous splash clears and an observer’s screen with color removed.

## 6. Visible Snow Rocket burst (HIT-06)

- [ ] 6.1 Show the area reached by an actual rocket detonation using the defaults in the matching requirement. Record a rocket hitting the floor near two visitors and one expiring in flight. Only the contact produces the ring.

## 7. Bankshot bounce ticks (HIT-07)

- [ ] 7.1 Mark the point where a Bankshot changes direction using the defaults in the matching requirement. Follow the existing Bankshot through three legal bounces and its terminal impact in slow playback.

## 8. A snow mark for a missed shot (HIT-08)

- [ ] 8.1 Show where a missed shot really ended using the defaults in the matching requirement. Compare a floor miss, an airborne timeout and a shot passing the decorative tree.

## 9. Who hit what and where (HIT-09)

- [ ] 9.1 Deliver enough confirmed contact information to identify its cause using the defaults in the matching requirement. Exercise the new records with two shooters, a three-pellet trigger and a blast touching multiple targets.

## 10. Quiet joins and one cue per impact (HIT-10)

- [ ] 10.1 Silence historical room effects and suppress repeated ones using the defaults in the matching requirement. Join with twelve prior bells and three clears, then deliver one new ricochet twice and check playback.

## 11. Tune in play and verify controls

- [ ] 11.1 Compare pistol hits, accepted ricochets and rocket bursts in solo, then repeat with two players shooting different targets. Inspect a miss and a late join at normal speed and in single frames.
- [ ] 11.2 Switch off Marks, Sounds and Detail separately. Check a grayscale view, touch at 390 CSS px and a stereo view with no DOM overlay. Counts and collision results must match the control run.
- [ ] 11.3 Run the affected C and Lua unit suites with their separate 95% gates and the relevant automated playthroughs. Record measured cues, negative cases and platform or device gaps with the change.
