The Lantern Hall 0.6.0 rebuilds Door 1 around direct gunplay. Five original animated weapons carry the player from a neon cinema and diner to the launch gantries, lunar hive and Moon Bowl. Shoot the FIZZ cans for a crowd clear, slide the frozen skater into the till and finish the Queen with the twin launchers.

The client and dedicated room now use the `tinseltown-arcade-1` mission revision. Linux hosts should update their room service to 0.6.0 for co-op. Saved ornaments carry over.
